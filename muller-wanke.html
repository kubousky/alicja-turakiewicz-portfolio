mÜller&wanke

2022

As part of my Digital Designer course, I was tasked with creating a comprehensive web and mobile design solution for Müller&Wanke, a high-end housing agency serving the Tri-City area. The primary goals were to facilitate the rental, purchase, and sales processes for clients, increase engagement with the company's agents, and convey the luxurious nature of Müller&Wanke's services.

The design features a clean, modern aesthetic with a focus on light, airy layouts and high-quality photography showcasing the agency's premium properties. Key user flows include browsing available listings, viewing detailed property information, and scheduling visits with agents - all optimized for both desktop and mobile experiences.

I am proud to have created a solution that elevates the Müller&Wanke brand while streamlining the user experience for both prospective buyers/renters and the agency's sales team.

TOOLS:
To emphasize Müller&Wanke's exclusivity, I incorporated subtle design elements like muted color palettes, elegant typography, and minimalist interfaces. This allows the stunning property visuals to take center stage, immersing users in the luxury lifestyle the agency represents.
The project was well-received, with course instructor Tom Biskup commending the "beautiful, light layouts" and noting that the design was "significantly above average."
