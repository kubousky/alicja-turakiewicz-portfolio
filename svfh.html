SVFH - VALENCIAN SOCIETY OF HOSPITAL PHARMACY
2021 - 2023

Working with SVFH, a scientific association in Valencia's healthcare sector, I developed comprehensive visual identities for their professional events during 2021-2023. The project encompassed branding, graphic design, and social media assets for multiple high-profile events, including the SVFH Congress and specialized medical conferences.

All designs respected medical context while introducing fresh, engaging visual elements, what contributed to strengthening SVFH's position as a leading voice in hospital pharmacy practice.

WEBINAR OF RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS
2021

For this online event, I designed materials featuring a raised fist symbol. This graphic represented both a common exercise for managing Rheumatoid Arthritis as well as a symbol of patient empowerment. I supported this with an expressive color palette of turquoise and purple, creating a professional aesthetic suitable for the webinar's medical content.
